Transitive dependencies carry their own notices in the installed distributions.
No model, font, mask, TV art, logo, sound or third-party character is bundled in
Phase A. Text uses installed system fonts with Qt's fallback. Signal-loss graphics
are drawn by the application. The developer's test pattern is generated by
FFmpeg's `testsrc2` and is not a production fallback.

YuNet, MediaPipe and OFL font/model notices must be added when their respective
phases start; no licence clearance or implementation is claimed for absent assets.
